About Us

Where Beauty Meets Science

Artistry Clinics Cunningham Road is Bangalore’s premier aesthetic destination, led by certified dermatologists and specialist practitioners. We offer a complete range of skin, hair, lash, and cosmetic treatments — each tailored precisely to your needs.

Located on Cunningham Road opposite Fortis Hospital, we blend cutting-edge technology with deeply personalised care. Whether you’re seeking a subtle refresh or a complete transformation, our results consistently exceed expectations.

Serving Bangalore through two premium locations — Artistry Clinics Cunningham Road and Artistry Clinics Lux — both held to the same uncompromising standard of excellence.
